Why tail insurance matters

Most employed physicians have claims-made malpractice coverage. When you leave a job, you need "tail" coverage for claims filed later about patients you already treated. Your contract decides who pays for it, and many new doctors only find out when they resign.

Every finding in your report points to the exact clause it comes from, so you can check each one yourself. Our review playbooks were built with healthcare employment attorneys from more than 3,000 physician contracts, and our contracts team spot-checks every report before it is sent.

Who can see my contract?

Your file is encrypted with AES-256 in transit and at rest, and RedlineMD follows SOC 2, ISO 27001 and GDPR practices. Only you can see your report. We delete your contract 30 days after delivery, or right away if you ask.
